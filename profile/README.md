# Lutta

**The social training app for jiu-jitsu.**

Train. Record. Connect. Achieve.

---

Every BJJ training journal ever built has died of manual entry. Strava works because
GPS logs the run for free. Jiu-jitsu has no sensor, so the question is not what the log
should look like. It is what fills the log without the user filling it.

Lutta's answer is that **every roll has two people in it.** One of them being diligent
is enough for both.

That makes the roll, not the session, the atom of the product. It also makes every tag
a claim about another person, which is why the whole product is built on consent:
nothing lands on your log until you say it happened, and nobody publishes your name
without your approval.
